PitchdleUnder the hood

From a headline to a call

1FindLive mode searches Hacker News and Google News by field and country. An AI screen keeps only real startups and files the rest with the reason.
2FillEach tracked startup's money raised, investors, stages, vision and evidence are filled from its website and news, only from what the sources say.
3CheckOne small AI call checks the card players will see: an operating startup, a pitch that says what it does, nothing misleading.
4ReviewPlayers rate it in the daily game; A'Pelago, Claude, GPT, Grok and social media review it on the same four calls.
5DecideThe radar gives the call, the readiness levels, thesis fit, ranked next steps and the next move.

Honest by design

  • Unknown stays unknown: scores use only evidenced factors, and every startup shows its coverage.
  • Tech readiness comes from pilots and deployments, never from a startup's own claim.
  • Evidence estimated by AI is marked as such, and a startup is never called Back until the host confirms it.
  • A social media read only counts posts the model actually cited on that platform.
  • Investors are vetted by moderators, and guests can never outvote members.
  • The game never waits on the web or on an AI model.

Measured against the evidence

  • Ocean Impact: measurability, problem pressure, solution potential.
  • Business Readiness (KTH BRL): market, value proposition, business model, scalability.
  • Technology Readiness: TRL 1–9, from evidence.
  • Investment: Redstone's ten factors, from team and market to financials and deal dynamics.
  • The engine maps them onto the players' four calls, so crowd and evidence meet on one scale.
