# Pitchdle: 2-minute video script

About 250 spoken words, roughly 1:50 at a calm pace, which leaves room for pauses. Each scene says what to show on
screen and what to say over it.

The gap scene uses the demo data. TideLedger's and Rakko Feed's evidence scores (1.7 and 4.0) and their calls (Avoid,
Back) don't change. Their crowd scores move a little with how many demo answers there are, so the script leaves those
to the screen.

## Before you record

- Use Demo mode. Add demo answers under Game → Next games → Demo answers, so the crowd numbers show. With the
  OpenRouter key set, the AI reviews are in too.
- Use a 1080p browser window at about 110% zoom, light theme, with the bookmarks bar hidden. Record the game in a
  phone-sized window.

## Script

### 0:00–0:09 · Hook

**Screen:** The game card for Rakko Feed: *"Dried Baltic seaweed in cow feed. The cows burp less methane."*

**Say:** "Would you back this? Funds and accelerators answer that every week, often from a pitch deck and a gut
feeling."

### 0:09–0:23 · What it is

**Screen:** The Dashboard grid, with its Back, Watch and Avoid labels.

**Say:** "Pitchdle is an ocean opportunity radar for Redstone and A'Pelago. For every startup it gives a call — back,
watch or avoid — says why, and who they should talk to next."

### 0:23–0:45 · The game

**Screen:** /play on a phone. Tap the four rows, press LOCK IT IN, then show the results card with the "Everyone" bars.

**Say:** "It starts with a daily game. Everyone gets the same five pitches and rates each on four calls — novel,
useful, plausible, investable — from zero to five, then sees how everyone else read it. Guests play in seconds; vetted
investors see the full card and count double."

### 0:45–1:04 · The radar

**Screen:** Open Rakko Feed's Details. Move slowly across Readiness, Thesis fit, Next steps and The call.

**Say:** "It all lands on the radar: four readiness levels — ocean impact, business, technology and investment — fit
with A'Pelago's and Redstone's theses, and ranked next steps: investors, grants, partners and clients, with one next
move aimed at the weakest level."

### 1:04–1:24 · The gap

**Screen:** TideLedger's Perception gap line, then Rakko Feed's.

**Say:** "And the gap between crowd and evidence is the signal. In our demo, the crowd loves TideLedger's pitch, but
its evidence scores 1.7: avoid. Rakko Feed underwhelms the crowd, yet scores 4.0: back — exactly the startup a
gut-feel process misses."

### 1:24–1:48 · Under the hood

**Screen:** Switch to Live and run Find startups. Open + Add startup, then show a card filling in.

**Say:** "In live mode it finds real ocean startups in the news, or you add one by hand, and AI fills each card only
from what its sources say. A'Pelago's own reviewers, Claude, GPT, Grok and social media all rate it on the same four
calls — and nothing is called back until a human confirms the evidence."

### 1:48–1:58 · Close

**Screen:** The Pitchdle title, or the poster's "Would you back this?"

**Say:** "Pitchdle: the crowd's gut feeling, checked against the evidence, turned into a decision. Thank you."

## Recording tips

- Record the voice first, then cut the screen clips to fit it. That's easier than talking while clicking.
- Leave a one-second pause after "avoid" and "back" in the gap scene. That's the moment to land.
- If you run long, shorten the "Under the hood" part first. The game, the radar and the gap are the story.
